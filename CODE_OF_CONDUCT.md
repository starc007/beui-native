# Code of Conduct

beUI Native follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, welcome people of every background and experience level, give and accept feedback graciously, and focus on what's best for the project and its community. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behavior that breaks this code, contact the maintainer, [@starc007](https://github.com/starc007), privately on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
